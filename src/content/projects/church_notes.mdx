Church Notes is a focused note-taking app that integrates relevant Bible verses into your notes. Whether you’re studying, journaling, or capturing an idea, it can bring in verses from different Bible translations without interrupting your writing flow.

[![Get Church Notes on Google Play](/images/get_it_on_google_play.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lonedreamstudio.church_notes)

## Product details

- A rich text editor for structured notes.
- Automatic Bible verse insertion.
- Local-first storage for fast, dependable access.
- Support for multiple Bible translations.

## Tech stack

- **Flutter** for the mobile experience.
- **SQLite** for local note storage.
- **Quill** for rich text editing.

The source is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Salvist/church_notes).
