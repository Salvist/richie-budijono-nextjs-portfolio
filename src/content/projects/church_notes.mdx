Church Notes helps people capture ideas, study notes, and journal entries while keeping relevant Bible verses close to their writing. It combines focused rich-text editing with local storage and support for multiple translations.

## Product capabilities

- Write and format structured notes in a focused rich-text editor.
- Bring relevant Bible verses directly into a note without interrupting the writing flow.
- Work with Bible passages from multiple translations.
- Keep notes available locally for dependable offline access.

## Tech stack

- **Flutter** provides the Android interface and shared application logic.
- **SQLite** persists notes locally for offline access.
- **Quill** powers the structured rich-text editing experience.
