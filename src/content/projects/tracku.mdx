TrackU reduces manual expense entry and turns everyday financial records into reports that are easier to understand.

## Product capabilities

- Scan receipts and extract transaction details with AI-assisted document processing.
- Organize income, expenses, categories, and merchants in one place.
- Review weekly and monthly reports to understand spending patterns.
- Turn receipts and statements into structured financial records with less manual entry.

## Tech stack

- **Flutter** provides the Android interface and shared expense-tracking workflows.
- **Firebase** supports persisted application data and cloud services.
- **AI document scanning** extracts structured transaction details from receipts and statements.
