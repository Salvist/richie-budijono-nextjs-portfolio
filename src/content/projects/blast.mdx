## The problem

BLAST helps kids get better placement in bilingual English and Spanish programs. When I joined, they had a prototype and a vision — but no production app, no data platform, and thousands of students who needed both.

## What I built

I took the prototype to a full production mobile app in under 3 months for iOS, and another 3 months for Android, publishing both to the App Store and Google Play.

From there, the product grew into a platform:

- **A data analytics solution** that streamlined performance tracking for 8,000+ students across 40+ schools, giving educators actionable insights and driving data discussions throughout the school year.
- **AI-assisted assessment** — I integrated Google Speech-to-Text with custom tweaks to transcribe student audio, reaching 90% accuracy and reducing manual checking by 70%.
- **Automated PDF reporting** covering schools, classrooms, and individual students across an entire year, now used by 15+ school administrators to send reports directly to parents.
- **ClassLink SSO integration** so schools could onboard students without manual account creation, matching third-party identity data to the platform's school model.

## The results

What started as a prototype is now the system of record for bilingual assessment at dozens of schools. The platform's usage led to measurably increased engagement in data discussions between teachers and administrators throughout the school year.
