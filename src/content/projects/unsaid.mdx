Unsaid offers one thoughtful question each day to make personal reflection and meaningful conversation easier to begin.

[![Get Unsaid on Google Play](/images/get_it_on_google_play.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lonedreamstudio.unsaid)

## Product capabilities

- Discover a new question designed for daily reflection.
- Use focused prompts to explore thoughts that may otherwise go unspoken.
- Share questions as starting points for more meaningful conversations.

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter** provides the Android interface and daily prompt experience.
- **Firebase** supports the app's persisted data and cloud services.
