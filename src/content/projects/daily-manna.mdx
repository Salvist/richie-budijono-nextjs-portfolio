## Product capabilities

- Choose a daily chapter goal and continue sequentially through the Bible from a saved reading position.
- Read in a focused Scripture view with multi-color highlights, bookmarks, and personal notes.
- Follow reading history through daily streaks, monthly activity heatmaps, progress summaries, and achievements.
- Set reading reminders, view Scripture and progress in home-screen widgets, and manually log reading completed outside the app.
- Use Manna Mode to block selected distracting apps until the day's Bible-reading goal is complete.

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter** provides the shared mobile interface and core reading experience for iOS and Android.
- **Firebase** supports authentication, persisted reading data, messaging, analytics, and cloud services.
- **Swift** implements iOS-specific widgets and Screen Time integrations used by Manna Mode.
- **Kotlin** implements Android-specific widgets, notifications, and app-blocking integrations.
- **Next.js** powers the public product website and its app-store download journey.
