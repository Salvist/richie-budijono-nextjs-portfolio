Expense Archive is a mobile experiment for tracking expenses offline and understanding spending by category and over time.

## Product capabilities

- Record expenses without requiring a network connection.
- Organize spending by category and business.
- Review analytics and trends across recorded expenses.
- Keep financial records persisted locally on the device.

## Screenshots

![Expense Archive home screen showing locally tracked spending](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Salvist/expense_archive/refs/heads/master/screenshots/home_page.png)

## Tech stack

- **Flutter** provides the mobile interface and expense-tracking workflows.
- **Realm** stores expense records locally for offline-first use.
