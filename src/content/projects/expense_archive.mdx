Expense Archive is a mobile experiment for tracking expenses offline and understanding spending by category and over time.

## Product capabilities

- Fast offline expense entry.
- Category and business organization.
- Spending analytics and trends.
- Local data persistence.

## Tech stack

- **Flutter** for the mobile application.
- **Realm** for offline-first storage.

More screenshots and source code are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Salvist/expense_archive/tree/master/screenshots).
