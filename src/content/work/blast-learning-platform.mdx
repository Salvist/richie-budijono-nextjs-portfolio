## The context

BLAST needed more than a mobile experience. Its educators and administrators also needed dependable tools for tracking student performance, discussing data, and sharing useful reports with families.

## My role

I helped take the mobile product from prototype to production, shipping the iOS release in under three months and following with Android. As the platform expanded, my work grew across product engineering, data workflows, AI-assisted transcription, reporting, and third-party identity.

## What I worked on

- Built product experiences for students, educators, and administrators.
- Developed an analytics platform used across more than 40 schools.
- Integrated and tuned speech-to-text workflows for student recordings.
- Built year-long PDF reporting for school, classroom, and student performance.
- Integrated ClassLink SSO while accounting for school and account matching.

## Outcomes

The platform supports more than 8,000 students. The transcription workflow reached approximately 90% accuracy and reduced manual checking by about 70%, while the reporting system became part of the workflow for more than 15 school administrators.

## What this work demonstrates

Successful product engineering is rarely a single feature. It means understanding the people using the system, finding the handoffs that slow them down, and building software that stays useful as the product grows.
