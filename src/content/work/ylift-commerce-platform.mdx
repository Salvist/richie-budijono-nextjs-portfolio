## The context

Y Lift was building both a customer-facing e-commerce experience and the internal systems needed to support day-to-day operations. The front end had to move quickly without turning every new screen into one-off code.

## My role

I led front-end delivery while collaborating closely with design and back-end engineering. My responsibility included translating designs into production interfaces, improving the component architecture, and making the codebase easier for other developers to extend.

## What I worked on

- Delivered more than 40 pages across commerce and internal workflows.
- Built over 100 reusable components and widgets.
- Migrated and refactored legacy code for readability and maintainability.
- Worked with the back-end team to clarify API contracts and missing data.
- Helped keep behavior and data consistent across multiple product surfaces.

## Outcome

The shared component foundation supported the main e-commerce experience and three internal applications. It also gave the team a more maintainable base for adding new workflows without rebuilding the same patterns repeatedly.

## What this work demonstrates

Fast delivery and maintainable systems are not opposites. A practical component strategy, clear API conversations, and attention to the people maintaining the code make both possible.
