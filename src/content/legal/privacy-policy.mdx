This privacy policy applies to mobile applications and related services
published by Lone Dream Studio (collectively, the "Applications"). The
Applications are provided as freemium services and are intended for use "AS
IS."

## Information Collection and Use

The information an Application collects depends on the Application and the
features you use. Automatically collected information may include:

- Your device's Internet Protocol address, such as its IP address
- The pages or screens you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time
  spent on them
- The time spent using an Application
- The operating system used on your mobile device

The Applications do not gather precise information about the location of your
mobile device.

Lone Dream Studio may use information you provide to contact you with important
information, required notices, or marketing communications. When needed to
provide an Application's features, Lone Dream Studio may ask you for personally
identifiable information, including an email address or user ID. Requested
information is retained and used as described in this policy.

## Third-Party Access

Aggregated and anonymized data may be periodically transmitted to external
services to help Lone Dream Studio improve the Applications and related
services. Lone Dream Studio may share information with third parties only in
the ways described in this policy.

Depending on the Application and the features you use, an Application may use
third-party services that maintain their own privacy policies:

- [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

Lone Dream Studio may disclose user-provided and automatically collected
information:

- As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal
  process
- When Lone Dream Studio believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary
  to protect its rights, protect your safety or the safety of others,
  investigate fraud, or respond to a government request
- With trusted service providers who work on Lone Dream Studio's behalf, do not
  independently use the information disclosed to them, and have agreed to
  follow the rules described in this policy

## Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by an Application by uninstalling
it. You may use the standard uninstall process available on your mobile device
or through the applicable mobile application marketplace.

## Data Retention Policy

Lone Dream Studio retains user-provided data for as long as you use the
Applications and for a reasonable time afterward. To request deletion of data
you provided through an Application, email
[lonedreamstudio@gmail.com](mailto:lonedreamstudio@gmail.com). Lone Dream Studio
will respond within a reasonable time.

## Children

The Applications are not directed to anyone under the age of 13. Lone Dream
Studio does not knowingly solicit data from, market to, or collect personally
identifiable information from children under 13.

If Lone Dream Studio discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal
information, that information will be deleted from its servers. If you are a
parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information,
contact [lonedreamstudio@gmail.com](mailto:lonedreamstudio@gmail.com).

## Security

Lone Dream Studio is committed to safeguarding the confidentiality of your
information and uses physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect
the information it processes and maintains.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Lone Dream Studio will
notify users of changes by publishing the updated policy on this page. You are
advised to review this policy regularly. Continued use of an Application after
changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Your Consent

By using an Application, you consent to the processing of your information as
described in this privacy policy and as amended by Lone Dream Studio.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about privacy while using an Application or about Lone
